Our Story

Mere father ne 1980s ke aakhir mein Begusarai, Bihar mein ek chhota sa tailoring stall shuru kiya tha. Bachpan se hi main unke saath rehta tha — school ke baad kaafi waqt wahi guzarta, floor se pins uthata, machine mein dhaga daalna seekhta aur dheere-dheere tailoring ki duniya ko samajhne laga. Sach kahun toh bicycle chalana seekhne se pehle maine sewing machine mein dhaga daalna seekh liya tha. Jab main 16 saal ka hua, father ne mujhe customers ki trousers ki hemming jaise chhote kaam dene shuru kar diye. Aur agar crease bilkul seedhi nahi hoti thi, toh uski correction sirf shop par nahi, dinner table par bhi hoti thi. 😄 2011 mein maine Gohana Road, Sonipat par Tailor Point ki shuruaat ki — sirf do machines aur ek simple principle ke saath: har suit us insaan ke liye tailor hona chahiye jo use pehnega, na ki sirf kisi size chart ke hisaab se.

Aaj 15 saal se zyada ho gaye hain. Hum 1,000 se bhi zyada suits stitch kar chuke hain aur shop bhi pehle se kaafi grow ho gayi hai. Lekin ek cheez aaj bhi bilkul nahi badli — perfect fit ke liye hamari commitment. Mera maanna hai ki ek achha suit woh hota hai jo pehli baar pehenne par hi naturally fit lage — jisme baar-baar adjustment ki zarurat na pade. Ye wahi standard hai jo mere father ne mujhe sikhaya tha. Aaj bhi har customer, har measurement aur har order ke saath main usi standard ko follow karta hoon. Tailor Point — jahan har suit sirf stitch nahi hota, aapke liye tailor kiya jata hai.
